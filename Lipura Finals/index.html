Welcome to FaithBeat

Experience music in a new way! FaithBeat lets every musician control the sound of each instrument, add music, use a built-in metronome, and communicate with other musicians using talkback — all through Bluetooth or Wi-Fi.

FaithBeat Mission

Empowering Every Musician

FaithBeat’s mission is to give every musician whether a student, worship member, or band player an affordable and simple way to hear themselves clearly and play in sync with others.

Connect On Any Device

FaithBeat connects up to 20 musicians using Bluetooth or Wi-Fi, with no internet needed. Practice or perform anywhere while staying perfectly in time together.

How FaithBeat Works

FaithBeat uses AI technology to detect and separate sounds like guitar, bass, drums, and keyboard from any song. Each musician can adjust volume levels, hear only what they need, and practice in sync even without expensive equipment.

Using Bluetooth or Wi-Fi, one phone acts as the host, connecting up to 20 devices. The app minimizes delay, ensuring clear and real-time sound for everyone.

Key FaithBeat Features

Individual Sound Control

Adjust the volume of each instrument easily — guitar, bass, drums, or keyboard — directly from your phone.

AI Sound Separation

FaithBeat’s AI detects and separates instruments automatically, giving you clean, adjustable tracks for perfect practice.

Wireless Group Connection

Connect up to 20 devices through Wi-Fi or Bluetooth and play together in real time without internet data.

Talkback Feature

Use your headset mic to talk with other musicians perfect for communication during rehearsals and performances.
